Correspondence Guidance

Before Contacting LRI

To support a productive scientific or professional discussion, please include the information relevant to your inquiry.

  • Your name and institutional affiliation, if applicable.
  • A brief description of your research interest or technical challenge.
  • The relevant LRI publication, research area, or engineering initiative.
  • Your proposed objectives and expected outcomes, particularly for collaboration inquiries.
  • Any important deadlines or scheduling requirements.

Privacy and Information Handling

Please do not include confidential, proprietary, export-controlled, personally sensitive, or security-restricted information in an initial inquiry. Discuss appropriate information-sharing arrangements before exchanging restricted technical materials.
