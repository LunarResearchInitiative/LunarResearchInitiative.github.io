Applied Research and Knowledge Development

Transforming Research Into Engineering Practice

Developing practical frameworks, technical resources, and educational initiatives that connect scientific research with the engineering challenges of lunar and aerospace operations.

Research Into Practice

Our Initiatives

LRI initiatives translate scientific knowledge into structured engineering methods, technical references, educational resources, and practical decision-support concepts.

These initiatives connect research findings with the needs of engineers, researchers, mission planners, and organizations preparing for sustained lunar operations.

The portfolio includes published resources alongside research and development directions that require further investigation, verification, and refinement.

Quality Architecture

Unified Lunar Quality Framework Handbook Series

A technical resource initiative that seeks to organize the principles of the Unified Lunar Quality Framework into structured engineering references for lunar operations.

Development Focus
  • Integrated lunar quality architectures
  • Verification and evidence integrity
  • Operational timing and decision authority
  • Quality requirements and mission assurance

Intended output: Technical handbooks and structured framework references for engineering applications.

Risk and Mission Assurance

Lunar Risk Management System

The Lunar Risk Management System (LRMS) explores structured approaches for connecting mission hazards, quality controls, engineering evidence, and operational constraints.

Development Focus
  • Mission risk identification and assessment
  • Risk-based quality controls
  • Communication delay and changing hazards
  • Evidence-supported risk decisions

Intended output: Research-based risk management methods and analytical guidance for lunar systems.

Decision Science

Decision Quality and Authority Under Delay

Practical research resources that examine how evidence, timing, uncertainty, and decision authority interact in high-risk and remotely supervised environments.

Resource Focus
  • Decision quality in high-risk environments
  • Decision authority under communication delay
  • Evidence freshness and decision relevance
  • Accountability and escalation concepts

Available resources: Open technical guides and related research outputs in the LRI publications collection.

Evidence and Verification

Verification and Qualification Methods

A research translation initiative that examines how engineering requirements and quality characteristics connect to verification activities, supporting evidence, and defensible qualification claims.

Development Focus
  • Critical-to-quality identification
  • CTQ-to-verification structuring
  • Evidence traceability and readiness
  • Testbed fidelity and qualification boundaries

Intended output: Structured verification planning methods and technical guidance for evaluating qualification evidence.

Scope and Development

LRI distinguishes established research outputs from proposed engineering resources and ongoing development concepts. The inclusion of an initiative does not imply completed validation, formal qualification, regulatory approval, or commercial availability.

Knowledge Development

From Scientific Research to Practical Application

LRI uses a structured approach to connect scientific investigation with potential engineering applications.

01

Scientific Research

Identify engineering questions, examine available evidence, and develop scientifically grounded research concepts.

02

Method Development

Translate research findings into structured frameworks, analytical methods, technical definitions, and engineering guidance.

03

Evaluation and Refinement

Examine assumptions, supporting evidence, applicability, limitations, and verification requirements before advancing technical claims.

04

Knowledge Dissemination

Share research contributions through scientific publications, technical resources, educational materials, and professional engagement.
