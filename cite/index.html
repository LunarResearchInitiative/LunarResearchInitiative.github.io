Scientific Attribution and Referencing

Citation Information

Recommended scholarly citation formats and referencing guidance for Lunar Research Initiative publications, technical resources, research frameworks, and institutional website content.

Research Integrity

How to Reference LRI

When referencing information from the Lunar Research Initiative website, identify the relevant webpage or resource and provide sufficient bibliographic information for readers to locate the original source.

When referencing a specific scientific publication, cite the original journal article, book, repository record, or technical document rather than the general LRI website.

Use the Digital Object Identifier (DOI) whenever one exists. Consult the official publisher or repository record for the authoritative bibliographic information.

Recommended Reference Formats

Citing the LRI Homepage

Use the following examples when your work specifically references content on the LRI homepage. For an individual webpage, substitute that page's title, URL, and publication or revision date, if available.

A general mention of the entire website may not require a formal reference entry, depending on your citation style.

Reference Access Date

Change the date to match when you consulted the website. Applicable citations update automatically.

APA 7th Edition

Author-Date

Common in academic research, behavioral sciences, education, and interdisciplinary studies.

Lunar Research Initiative. (n.d.). Lunar Research Initiative. https://lunarresearchinitiative.github.io/

IEEE

Numbered Reference

Widely used in engineering, computer science, telecommunications, and technical research.

Lunar Research Initiative, “Lunar Research Initiative.” [Online]. Available: https://lunarresearchinitiative.github.io/. [Accessed: Month Day, Year].

Chicago

Bibliography

Useful for scholarly publications, historical analysis, humanities, and interdisciplinary research.

Lunar Research Initiative. “Lunar Research Initiative.” Accessed Month Day, Year. https://lunarresearchinitiative.github.io/.

Harvard

Author-Date

An author-date referencing approach. Formatting conventions may vary among universities and publishers.

Lunar Research Initiative (n.d.) Lunar Research Initiative. Available at: https://lunarresearchinitiative.github.io/ (Accessed: Day Month Year).
Reference Management

BibTeX Citation

Import the following reference into a compatible reference manager or LaTeX bibliography. 

@misc{LRI_website,
  author       = {{Lunar Research Initiative}},
  title        = {Lunar Research Initiative},
  howpublished = {\url{https://lunarresearchinitiative.github.io/}},
  note         = {Accessed: Month Day, Year}
}

These examples use an undated website reference because the homepage does not identify a formal publication or revision date. Follow the specific requirements of your institution, journal, or citation style.

Scientific Integrity

Responsible Scholarly Referencing

01

Cite the Original Source

Reference the primary publication or archived technical resource supporting the specific scientific or engineering claim.

02

Use Persistent Identifiers

Prefer DOIs and other persistent identifiers when available to support reliable identification and retrieval.

03

Identify the Correct Version

Distinguish preprints, accepted manuscripts, versions of record, revised technical guides, and version-specific repository deposits.

04

Preserve Scientific Context

Represent research findings accurately and retain the assumptions, limitations, and applicability conditions relevant to the cited contribution.

05

Verify Bibliographic Details

Confirm publication titles, authors, publication dates, journal details, pagination, and persistent identifiers against authoritative records.

06

Respect Publication Rights

Follow applicable copyright, licensing, attribution, and publisher requirements when reproducing figures, tables, excerpts, or technical materials.

Copyright and Attribution

Citation does not automatically grant permission to reproduce copyrighted material. Consult the license or rights statement associated with each publication, repository record, illustration, or technical resource before reproducing or distributing its content.
