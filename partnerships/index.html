Shared Scientific Objectives

Why Collaborate With LRI?

Sustained lunar exploration presents interconnected scientific and engineering challenges that extend across traditional disciplinary boundaries. Quality engineering, systems engineering, verification, reliability, autonomy, and human decision-making all influence mission outcomes.

The Lunar Research Initiative welcomes opportunities to investigate these challenges with organizations and researchers who bring complementary expertise, operational knowledge, analytical capabilities, and technical perspectives.

LRI seeks collaborations that produce meaningful scientific contributions, strengthen engineering methods, and advance the evidence supporting future lunar operations.

Establishing Collaboration

How to Begin

The following process provides a starting point for exploring potential scientific and engineering collaborations.

01

Initial Contact

Introduce your organization, research interests, technical challenge, and potential collaboration objectives.

02

Scientific Alignment

Identify shared research questions, complementary expertise, and opportunities for meaningful scientific contributions.

03

Define the Scope

Discuss research methods, responsibilities, available resources, expected outcomes, timelines, and applicable research requirements.

04

Establish an Agreement

Where appropriate, establish mutually acceptable arrangements addressing research responsibilities, data access, confidentiality, intellectual property, and dissemination.

Scientific Independence and Collaboration Principles

LRI approaches research collaboration through scientific integrity, transparent methodology, responsible evidence management, appropriate attribution, and respect for intellectual property.

Collaborative activities should preserve the independence of scientific conclusions and comply with applicable research ethics, confidentiality, contractual, and institutional requirements.

LRI does not act as a certification, regulatory, or approval authority. The collaboration opportunities described on this page do not imply an existing institutional affiliation, formal partnership, endorsement, funding commitment, or certification relationship.
