Editorial Purpose

Quality Beyond Compliance

Aerospace Quality Brief (AQB) examines the engineering and organizational conditions that determine whether quality systems genuinely protect performance, reliability, and operational safety.

Each edition connects practical quality engineering experience with broader questions involving evidence integrity, verification, configuration control, supplier behavior, corrective action, and decision authority.

AQB serves engineers, quality professionals, researchers, program leaders, and organizations seeking to improve the reliability of their decisions without introducing unnecessary procedural complexity.

Detection Does Not Prove Process Control

Aerospace Quality Brief

Teams Can Detect Nonconformance and Still Fail to Control the Process

June 30, 2026 Process Control Nonconformance Management

Inspection confirms whether a product meets a requirement at a particular point in time. Process control determines whether the system can repeatedly produce conforming output.

This edition examines why repeated detection can create false confidence when the underlying failure condition remains active, and why quality teams must distinguish detection effectiveness from process stability.

Editorial Coverage

Quality Signals That Matter Before Failure

AQB examines six interconnected engineering disciplines that influence operational reliability and decision quality.

01

Evidence Integrity

Evidence provenance, traceability, information freshness, transformation transparency, and confidence in engineering records.

02

Verification and Qualification

Test representativeness, verification boundaries, acceptance criteria, and the relationship between evidence and engineering claims.

03

Configuration Management

Engineering change, configuration drift, digital-thread integrity, and alignment between approved requirements and actual product configurations.

04

Supplier Quality and Risk

Supplier oversight, lower-tier visibility, outsourced processes, requirements flow-down, and supply-chain assurance.

05

Corrective Action and Process Control

CAPA effectiveness, recurrence prevention, process capability, meaningful metrics, and evidence of sustained improvement.

06

Decision and Mission Assurance

Decision authority, operational timing, uncertainty, escalation boundaries, accountability, and risk-informed engineering decisions.

Editorial Focus

Digital Thread and Evidence Integrity

Connected data creates value when engineering decisions remain traceable to evidence with identifiable origins, transformations, configurations, verification status, and decision authority. Faster information flow alone cannot guarantee more reliable decisions.
