Peer-Reviewed Research · Article in Press October 2026

Autonomous Evidence Integrity for Spaceflight: A Quality Assurance Framework for AI-Mediated Evidence Under Delayed Human Review in Lunar Operations

Ralph Figueroa

Journal of Space Safety Engineering · Elsevier · 2026

Introduces the Autonomous Evidence Integrity for Spaceflight (AEI-S) framework, which treats AI-mediated evidence as a distinct assurance object. The study establishes five evidence states and eighteen candidate quality-control data requirements addressing source traceability, transformation transparency, bounded decision use, and reconstruction after delayed human review. A retrospective mission analysis and an audit of eleven flight data-product specifications illustrate its potential application.

DOI: 10.1016/j.jsse.2026.10.004

Decision Freshness Boundaries Under Delayed Supervision in Lunar Surface Operations

Ralph Figueroa

Journal of the British Interplanetary Society · Volume 79, Issue 8, pp. 291–296

Develops a conceptual framework for determining when delayed supervisory input remains useful at local execution. The study distinguishes corrective, advisory, and historical supervisory roles according to information age and the rate of local state change. It proposes task-dependent decision-freshness boundaries for future empirical investigation and mission-rule development.

DOI: 10.59332/jbis-079-08-0291

Peer-Reviewed Journal Article August 2026

When Performance Persists Beyond Admissibility: A Comparative Modeling Study of Supervisory Autonomy Under Delay in Lunar Surface Operations

Ralph Figueroa

Journal of the British Interplanetary Society · Volume 79, Issue 8, pp. 280–290

Compares a simplified performance-only continuation policy with an assurance-bounded policy across 162 notional lunar operational cases. The analysis examines how communication delay, hazard drift, decision fidelity, mission consequence, and authority influence autonomous continuation. A preliminary proxy-based analysis of 60 public Perseverance cases supports limited threshold adjustment. The study distinguishes functional performance from justified operational continuation.

DOI: 10.59332/jbis-079-08-0280

Temporal Misalignment Under Delayed Supervision in Lunar Surface Operations

Ralph Figueroa

Journal of Space Safety Engineering · Volume 13, pp. 303–315

Introduces a timing-sensitive analytical method that combines hazard drift and decision fidelity into a temporal misalignment metric. Parametric Monte Carlo simulations examine how communication delay and hazard evolution jointly degrade supervisory relevance across low-tempo, transitional, and high-tempo lunar operational conditions.

DOI: 10.1016/j.jsse.2026.05.008

Peer-Reviewed Journal Article March 2026

Delay-Informed Decision Authority for Lunar Operations: A Unified Lunar Quality Framework

Ralph Figueroa

Journal of Space Safety Engineering · Volume 13, pp. 285–287

Presents the Unified Lunar Quality Framework as a delay-informed decision architecture linking evidence confidence, verification feasibility, hazard progression, and authority allocation. The framework defines analytical conditions for transferring authority among Earth-based supervision, local crew, and autonomous systems when operational timing constrains remote oversight.

DOI: 10.1016/j.jsse.2026.03.004
